About Steve

I'm Steve Lewey. For more than three decades, I've returned again and again to the book of Proverbs — not simply to study it, but to ask what its wisdom means when it meets real decisions, real relationships, real work, and real responsibility.

My background includes marketplace leadership, coaching, corporate chaplaincy, ministry leadership, and leadership development. I'm also a Maxwell Leadership Founding Partner and a trained facilitator with CBMC.

Today, my work centers on two things: helping people think more clearly and helping them apply enduring wisdom to the situations actually in front of them.

That may happen through writing and teaching, through the Ancient Intelligence framework, or through a confidential conversation as a Thinking Partner.

The Method

Ancient Intelligence is timeless wisdom made practical.

The world changes quickly. Human beings still face many of the same fundamental questions: Who can I trust? How should I decide? What am I building? What am I overlooking? When should I act? Whose counsel should I seek?

01 · READ

Encounter the Wisdom

Begin with enduring wisdom, especially the practical wisdom found in Proverbs.

02 · RECOGNIZE

Identify the Principle

Look beneath the ancient words to identify the principle at work.

03 · APPLY

Bring It Into Today

Ask what that principle means for the decision, relationship, work, or season in front of you.

Eight Areas of Application

01 · Character

Integrity

Living whole and bringing your stated values into alignment with your actual choices.

02 · Direction

Intentionality

Cultivating what matters rather than allowing circumstances to determine what grows.

03 · Action

Initiative

Recognizing when preparation has become procrastination and it is time to plow.

04 · Support

Environment

Building the relationships, habits, structures, and surroundings that support what you want to become.

05 · Wisdom

Counsel

Thinking with others rather than relying entirely on your own perspective.

06 · Perception

Discernment

Learning to see beneath the surface before deciding what something means.

07 · Influence

Speech

Recognizing how words build, damage, clarify, confuse, encourage, and influence.

08 · Perspective

Gratitude

Remembering what matters and developing a healthier perspective in difficult seasons.
